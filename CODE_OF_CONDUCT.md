# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Community participation must be respectful, constructive, and focused on the
project. Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, and publication of another person's private data are not accepted.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss technical decisions with evidence and civility.
- Respect maintainers, contributors, and users.
- Remove private data from logs and screenshots before sharing them.
- Accept review feedback professionally.
- Report security issues privately.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this standard. Repeated or severe violations may
result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.
